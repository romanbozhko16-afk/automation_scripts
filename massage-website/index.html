Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect at my first appointment?

Please arrive about 10 minutes early to fill out a brief health intake form. We'll talk about your goals and any problem areas, then you'll undress to your comfort level and relax under the sheet. You are always professionally draped — only the area being worked on is uncovered.

How do I book an appointment?

The easiest way is to call or text (555) 123‑4567. I'll usually respond within a couple of hours. Evening and weekend appointments are available on request.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash, all major credit/debit cards, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is due at the time of service. Gratuity is never expected but always appreciated.

What is your cancellation policy?

I ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Appointments canceled with less notice may be charged 50% of the service price; no-shows may be charged in full. Life happens — just communicate with me and we'll work it out.

Are there any reasons I shouldn't get a massage?

Massage isn't recommended if you have a fever, contagious illness, or certain acute medical conditions. If you're pregnant (first trimester) or have a serious health condition, please check with your doctor first. When in doubt, just ask — I'm happy to advise.

How often should I get a massage?

For general wellness, once a month works well for most people. For chronic pain or high stress, every 1–2 weeks brings the best results. We'll find a rhythm that fits your body and your budget.
