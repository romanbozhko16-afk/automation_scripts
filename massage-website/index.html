Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect at my first appointment?

Please arrive about ten minutes early for a brief health intake and consultation. We will discuss your goals and any areas of concern; you then undress to your comfort level and settle under the linens. You are professionally draped at all times — only the area being worked on is ever uncovered.

How do I reserve a session?

Call or text (509) 989‑2235 and I will respond personally, usually within a few hours. Evening and weekend appointments are available on request, and standing weekly or monthly reservations can be arranged.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash, all major credit and debit cards, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is due at the time of service. Gratuity is never expected and always appreciated.

What is your cancellation policy?

As each appointment reserves the studio exclusively for you, I ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Later cancellations may be charged 50% of the service; missed appointments may be charged in full.

Are there any reasons I should not receive massage?

Massage is not advised during fever or contagious illness, and certain medical conditions call for a physician's clearance — including first-trimester pregnancy. When in doubt, simply ask; I am glad to advise before you book.

How often should I schedule?

For general wellbeing, most guests find a monthly session ideal. For chronic tension or high demands, every one to two weeks brings the most lasting results. Many guests keep a standing reservation.
