Good to know

Questions, answered

Please arrive about ten minutes early for a brief health intake and consultation. You undress to your comfort level and settle under the linens — you are professionally draped at all times, and only the area being worked on is ever uncovered.

Call or text (509) 989-2235 and I’ll answer personally, usually within a few hours. Evening and weekend times are available on request, and standing weekly or monthly reservations can be arranged.

Cash, all major credit and debit cards, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is due at the time of service. Gratuity is never expected and always appreciated.

Each appointment reserves the studio exclusively for you, so I ask for at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Later cancellations may be charged 50% of the service; missed appointments may be charged in full.

Massage isn’t advised during fever or contagious illness, and some conditions call for a physician’s clearance — including first-trimester pregnancy. When in doubt, just ask before you book.

For general wellbeing, monthly works well for most guests. For chronic tension or a course of anti-cellulite massage, every one to two weeks brings the most lasting results.
